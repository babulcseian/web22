Md Babul Islam

Ph.D. Scholar · University of Calabria & University College Dublin

A research enthusiast who completed his Ph.D. with a fully funded scholarship at the School of ICT, University of Calabria (Italy), and University College Dublin (Ireland). He holds an M.Sc. in Information Engineering from Huzhou University, China, and a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from City University, Bangladesh.

He gained valuable experience as a lecturer at the Micro Institute of Technology (2018–2019) and later as a data scientist at Zhejiang Seating Technology in China. His research sits at the intersection of TinyML, Edge AI, Digital Twins, Smart Buildings, and Smart Agriculture.
